# Research notes: how MBTI is used in South Korea

> **Status:** raw desk research from a delegated research agent, 2026-10-23. Pass 1 mixed Korean and English queries; pass 2 was Korean-only. (KR) marks a Korean-language source. This pass couldn't reach Namu Wiki or Naver; a later pass read Namu Wiki through a real browser, see [`namu-wiki-korean.md`](namu-wiki-korean.md), which corrects the origin claim below. Not yet independently verified.

## 1. Social use, 2020~2026

- **Origin of the boom (corrected by the [Namu Wiki pass](namu-wiki-korean.md)):** this pass credited a 2020-06-13 episode of *Hangout with Yoo* (놀면 뭐하니?) where three celebrities tested their types (HIGH that the episode exists; https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/journalism-internship-mbti-profiling-offers-insights-an-icebreaker-and-maybe-even-love/10110572). The Namu Wiki pass found an MBTI craze already reported on 2020-06-08, built on a decade of school career tests, so the episode amplified the boom rather than starting it.
- **Reach:** Hankook Research (Dec 2021): over half of Koreans had taken a test; about 9 in 10 of those aged 19~28 (MEDIUM). https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/nation/2022/09/744_335673.html
- **The free test that lit it:** the free 16Personalities test is credited with the craze; the Korea MBTI Institute says it isn't real MBTI (KR, HIGH). https://www.segye.com/newsView/20220516517790
- **Dating:** about a third of dating-app profiles list a type (CNN, 2022-07-22; MEDIUM-HIGH, https://kvia.com/?p=1012789). One app, WIPPY, reported 92% of its users in their 20s list a type, ENFP most common at about 11% (KR, MEDIUM, https://v.daum.net/v/6XCl37at3Y). Thingsflow's "MBTI Blind Date" game had 1.2M downloads in week one (MEDIUM-HIGH, CNN link).
- **Etiquette:** asking each other's type early has become a kind of manners (KR, MEDIUM). https://www.bigissue.kr/magazine/new/284/1512
- **K-pop and variety:** idol groups introduce their types (NMIXX, 2022-02-07; KR, HIGH, https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202202081823013); Weverse ran a TXT × MBTI interactive (HIGH, https://magazine.weverse.io/article/view/393?lang=en); a 2022-03-19 *Hangout with Yoo* special split the cast into I and E teams, and re-tests flipped two "introverts" to E, to laughter (KR, HIGH, https://www.nocutnews.co.kr/news/5725768).
- **Hiring controversy:** shops and some companies asked for types in job ads or cover letters; the labour ministry said asking isn't illegal, while a workplace-rights lawyer called it problematic (KR, HIGH). https://v.daum.net/v/20220128140338643 · https://www.fnnews.com/news/202204021400424785

## 2. Meme and content formats

- **"너 T야?" ("are you a T?")**: from the *Fox Club* (폭스클럽) sketch series on the comedy channel 밈고리즘, video dated 2023-02-17. Excited F characters get deflated by a T character and ask "are you a T?"; people riff with finger "T"s and puns, and brands joined in (Baskin Robbins Korea's Earl Grey "Are You Tea?") (KR, HIGH). https://v.daum.net/v/20230815234812392 · https://www.newsis.com/view/NISX20230523_0002313264 · https://asianews.network/are-you-a-t-is-a-new-put-down-in-south-korea/
- **Situation-reaction skits** (유형별 반응): creators act out how each type reacts to a line such as "I was down, so I cut my hair"; comments map friends onto types (KR, HIGH, https://www.bigissue.kr/magazine/new/284/1512). Webtoon-style channels run T-vs-F and J-vs-P clash episodes (KR, MEDIUM, https://brunch.co.kr/@garnetberry/56).
- **The "bought bread" quiz:** "I was sad, so I bought bread." An F asks why you were sad; a T asks what bread (MEDIUM). https://hangling.beehiiv.com/p/english-240109-mbti
- **Self-labels:** 파워J (heavy planner), 쌉T (super T) (KR, MEDIUM, one blogger). https://brunch.co.kr/@misolinguide/204
- **Brands:** perfume and snack MBTI quizzes, ordering "types" in a delivery app (KR, MEDIUM). https://economist.co.kr/article/view/ecn202207160028

## 3. Compatibility (궁합) culture

- **Origin of the charts: unknown.** No source names a creator. Circulating charts contradict each other; one news chart lists ISTP as both best and worst for ESFJ (KR, HIGH, https://www.pinpointnews.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=147482). Zetawiki sorts them into about four families, only one tied to socionics-style "duality" logic (KR, MEDIUM, https://zetawiki.com/wiki/MBTI_%EA%B6%81%ED%95%A9%ED%91%9C_%EC%B4%9D%EC%A0%95%EB%A6%AC).
- **Pairs commonly called best:** ENFP–INFJ, ENFP–INTJ, INFJ–ENTP, INFP–ENFJ, ENTJ–INFP, ESTJ–ISFP/ISTP, ISTJ/ISFJ–ESFP/ESTP (MEDIUM; charts disagree). One playful tool scores ENFP + INFJ at 95% (HIGH, https://braindetox.kr/static/mbti_compatibility.html).
- **Pairs commonly called worst:** mostly S types against NF types.
- **Experts:** the Korea MBTI Institute says type alone can't judge compatibility (KR, HIGH, https://segye.com/newsView/20220506504031). One Korean test site withdrew its own accuracy claim and says chart grades may just be the author's rules (KR, HIGH, https://testmoa.com/mbti-compatibility/).

## 4. Nicknames and shorthand

- Hangul readings of the codes are used, e.g. 인프제 (INFJ), 앤팁 (ENTP) (KR, HIGH, economist.co.kr link above). Other readings not confirmed.
- Avoid: 16Personalities' names (NERIS branding) and Korean-side type names of unclear provenance (e.g. 성인군자형, 세상의 소금형). Korean MBTI rights are reportedly held by Assesta (어세스타) (MEDIUM).

## 5. Tone takeaways

- **Why it's fun:** a social lubricant; self-teasing; friends' quirks; the comfort of "someone like me" (HIGH, https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/opinion/20230627/obsession-with-mbti).
- **What drew criticism:** "너 T야?" stung T types and some teens felt excluded; some people now hide their type (KR, HIGH); rejecting people by type has cost relationships (KR, MEDIUM, https://kbmaeil.com/article/202305230362801); hiring screens.

## Researcher's suggestions for this toolkit

- Open with a one-line scenario in the "I bought bread" style; let the reaction hint at a lean, as a joke, not a verdict.
- Four-letter codes plus original persona names; say "MBTI-style".
- Frame pairs as a chemistry (케미) card: strengths, watch-outs, one tip. Label the chart "fun, author-made rules". Include same-type pairs.
- No "worst match" list: call hard pairings something like "extra effort".
- Shareable outputs: "how each persona reacts to X" cards with a catchphrase; J-vs-P banter.
- Personas tease themselves, never the user or other types.
- Let people say "I've changed" or "not sure"; show leanings as spectrums.
- Never for hiring or partner screening.
