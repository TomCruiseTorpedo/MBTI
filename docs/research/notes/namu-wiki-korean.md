> **Status:** raw desk research from a delegated research agent, 2026-10-23, done in Korean through a real browser after an earlier pass couldn't reach Namu Wiki. `NW` = https://namu.wiki/w/. Naver (search, 지식iN, blogs) is blocked in the built-in browser by a safety restriction, so Daum News stands in as the Korean news source. Not yet independently verified. This pass **corrects** one claim in [`korean-mbti-culture.md`](korean-mbti-culture.md) (the boom's origin).

## 1. Popularity and culture

- **Base before the boom:** Korea's MBTI institute dates from 1990; wide use among individuals grew through the 2010s, helped by school and career-education tests (NW/MBTI §8, HIGH).
- **Correction: the 2020-06-13 *Hangout with Yoo* (놀면 뭐하니?) episode amplified the boom; it didn't start it.** The episode is real (NW/놀면_뭐하니?/2020년, HIGH), but an 이코노미조선 piece dated 2020-06-08 already describes an MBTI craze (MBTI 열풍) (http://v.daum.net/v/20200608191334089, HIGH). The show returned to MBTI in a 2022 special.
- **Survey (Hankook Research, Dec 2021):** 38% know what MBTI is, 36% have heard of it; 80% of 18~29s know it vs 12% of 60+; 36% trust it, 35% don't; 83% of test-takers say their result fits (NW/MBTI §8, HIGH).
- **Replaced blood type** (혈액형 성격설) as the 2020s pop-psychology topic (NW/MBTI §5.2, HIGH).
- **Spoken shorthand:** codes are read as Hangul syllables, e.g. 잇티제 (ISTJ), 잇팁 (ISTP), 엔프피 (ENFP), with "TP" read as 팁 (NW/MBTI §9, HIGH).
- **"너 T야?":** from the 폭스클럽 sketches on the comedy channel 밈고리즘, where a blunt T-type cameraman gets called out by two F types (NW/너_T야? §1; NW/밈고리즘 §2.4, HIGH); filed by Namu as a 2023 meme (MEDIUM). Namu notes the "T = no empathy / psychopath" framing is a harmful prejudice (§4.1).
- **"우울해서 빵 샀어" ("I was sad, so I bought bread"):** a real T/F quiz meme (F asks why you're down, T asks what bread), later remixed; used on TV and by politicians 2023~2026 (NW/너_T야? §3, HIGH). Creator unknown.
- **"나 차 사고 났어" ("I crashed my car"):** a second common T/F quiz meme; T asks whether you're insured (보험 들었어?), F asks whether you're hurt. Confirmed in multiple Daum blog search results on 2026-10-23 (https://search.daum.net/search?w=blog&q=MBTI+차+사고+났어+보험+T+F), creator unknown (MEDIUM). Used as quiz question 8 in onboarding.
- **Cooling off:** the MBTI 일상툰 page notes the craze has cooled (NW/MBTI_일상툰 §4); a 2025 한국일보 piece says younger users moved on to hormone-based 에겐/테토 memes (MEDIUM).

## 3. Compatibility (궁합) charts

- Namu calls the theory behind one family of charts MBTI 관계론, i.e. socionics (사회인격학 / 소시오닉스), devised by Aušra Augustinavičiūtė; its "dual" relation is ideal and "conflict" is worst (NW/MBTI §2; NW/사회인격학, HIGH). Socionics letters aren't MBTI's, so mapping is approximate.
- **Socionics duals** listed on Namu type pages: ENTP–ISFJ, INTP–ESFJ, ENFP–ISTJ, INFP–ESTJ, ENFJ–ISTP. **Conflict:** ENTP–ISFP, ENFP–ISTP, ENTJ–ISFJ, ESTJ–INFJ, ESFJ–INTJ (MEDIUM).
- **The popular blog charts disagree:** ENFP–INFJ as 천생연분 (soulmates) appears in blog headlines with no socionics basis (NW/ENFP §3.2 footnote); the INFJ page names ENFP/ENTP as best (MEDIUM).
- **Criticism:** Namu's type pages carry disclaimers that socionics claims are unverified and shouldn't be applied to real relationships; NW/MBTI §5.2 calls compatibility posts not worth consulting (HIGH).
