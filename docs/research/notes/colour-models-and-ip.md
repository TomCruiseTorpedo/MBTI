# Research notes: colour models, Vinh Giang, and IP

> **Status:** raw desk research from a delegated research agent, 2026-10-23. Not legal advice. H = confirmed in a primary document the agent opened; M = secondary source or a primary seen only as a search snippet; L = folk or weak. Sources are numbered at the end.

## Lumina Spark

- 4 colours, 8 aspects, 24 qualities; each of 3 personas gets its own 24-quality profile (H, [1][2]).
- Aspects are 4 bipolar pairs: Extraverted/Introverted, People/Outcome Focused, Big Picture/Down to Earth, Inspiration/Discipline Driven (H, [1]).
- Colours average aspect pairs: Red = Extraverted + Outcome Focused; Yellow = Inspiration Driven + Big Picture; Green = Introverted + People Focused; Blue = Discipline Driven + Down to Earth (H, [2]).
- Built from Big Five research, with Jungian ideas folded in, rejecting "typing" (H, [3]).
- "Both/and": each pole scored separately, so a person can be high on both (H, [3][6]).
- Three personas: underlying (natural), everyday (how others see you), overextended (under stress) (H, [1][4]).
- Proprietary: the 144-item questionnaire, norms and portrait text, © Lumina Learning (H, [2][5]). Public: model overview, names, sample portrait.

## Other quadrant models

- **Insights Discovery:** Jung-based. Fiery Red = E+T, Sunshine Yellow = E+F, Earth Green = I+F, Cool Blue = I+T; opposite colours are forced-choice poles (H, [8][9]).
- **DISC:** Marston 1928 theory, never patented; Wiley's Everything DiSC from 2007 (H, [13]; M, [12]).
- **Thomas Erikson, *Surrounded by Idiots*:** Red/Yellow/Green/Blue paired with DISC, roughly Red = D, Yellow = i, Green = S, Blue = C (H, [14]; M, [15]). Swedish original 2014; heavily criticized [15].
- **True Colors:** Lowry, about 1978, Keirsey-based: Gold = SJ, Orange = SP, Green = NT, Blue = NF (M, [16][17][30]). **Its Blue and Green mean the opposite of DISC/Insights/Erikson colours.**
- **Hartman Color Code:** core motives (Red power, Blue intimacy, White peace, Yellow fun), not behaviour (M, [18]).
- **Social Styles:** Merrill & Reid; Driving / Expressive / Amiable / Analytical (M, [19]).

### Heuristic correspondence

| Quadrant | DISC / Erikson | Insights | Social Styles | MBTI letters (E/I × T/F) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outgoing + task | D / Red | Fiery Red | Driving | ENTJ, ESTJ, ENTP, ESTP |
| Outgoing + people | i / Yellow | Sunshine Yellow | Expressive | ENFP, ENFJ, ESFP, ESFJ |
| Reserved + people | S / Green | Earth Green | Amiable | INFJ, INFP, ISFJ, ISFP |
| Reserved + task | C / Blue | Cool Blue | Analytical | INTJ, INTP, ISTJ, ISTP |

Practitioner convention, not research: no peer-reviewed crosswalk between colour models was found. The E/I × T/F colour derivation ignores S/N and J/P.

## Vinh Giang

- The description says the system is based on what he learned from *Surrounded by Idiots*, so his colours are Erikson's DISC-derived Red/Yellow/Green/Blue (H, [20]). Upworthy coverage agrees (M, [21]).

## IP status (public record, not legal advice)

- **MBTI:** Myers-Briggs Type Indicator, Myers-Briggs, MBTI and related names are marks of the **Myers & Briggs Foundation, Inc.** The rules: use only for genuine MBTI products, as adjectives, and never reproduce the instrument (H, [27][28]). Full trademark guidelines PDF did not load. Nothing seen claims the four-letter codes; 16Personalities says the acronyms have no single owner-assigned definition (H, [25]).
- **16Personalities (NERIS Analytics):** claims copyright on its framework, profiles and content; registered NERIS marks; asks for brief quotes with attribution, not verbatim descriptions (H, [24][25]). No trademark filing found for the type nicknames (M-L, [26]). They also publish a page for AI use [24].
- **Insights:** © The Insights Group; INSIGHTS DISCOVERY word mark registered; no registration found for "Fiery Red" etc. (US records only) (H/M, [9][10][11]).
- **Lumina:** © Lumina Learning; no "Lumina Spark" trademark record found (UNVERIFIED).
- **DiSC:** Wiley claims "DiSC" and "Everything DiSC" (H, [13]). The plain "DISC" spelling is used by many vendors.
- **True Colors:** "True Colors®"; theory PDF says do not copy (M/H, [16]).
- **Social Styles:** TRACOM uses SOCIAL STYLE® [19].
- Apache-2.0 §6 grants no trademark rights [31].

## Researcher's design implications

1. Name the 16 personas in your own scheme; don't use 16Personalities' nicknames or group names (Analysts, Diplomats, Sentinels, Explorers).
2. Use the four-letter codes descriptively, with an "unaffiliated with the Myers & Briggs Foundation" acknowledgement. Keep "MBTI" and "Myers-Briggs" out of product and package names.
3. Treat colours as a derived view from two axes (outgoing/reserved, task/people) in an editable mapping file.
4. Write your own colour descriptors; avoid "Fiery Red", "Sunshine Yellow", "Commanding Red". Don't claim equivalence to any vendor model.
5. Lumina's structure (independent poles; natural / everyday / stressed levels) can be borrowed with your own names; not its quality names, items or text.
6. Write all persona text from scratch; link rather than copy.
7. S/N and J/P, which the colours drop, can drive level of detail and planning style.
8. Claim behaviour ("prompt styles for agents"), not science.
9. Add NOTICE and TRADEMARKS files; clear final names before launch.
