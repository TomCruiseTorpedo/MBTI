# Compatibility chart

> Generated by `scripts/build_compatibility.py`. Edit the rules there, then re-run it; don't edit this file by hand.

**For fun, author-made rules.** Like the 궁합 (compatibility) charts that circulate in Korea, this chart has no scientific basis. Its "perfect pair" rule echoes the 천생연분 (soulmate) pairs most often shared in Korean blog charts, such as ENFP–INFJ. Another family of Korean charts follows socionics, which calls complete opposites (such as ENTP–ISFJ) the ideal pair; in this chart those are Extra effort. The charts disagree, which is part of the fun. Types are never a reason to judge, hire or reject anyone.

## The rules

Compare two four-letter codes. The first rule that fits wins:

1. Same code: **Twins**.
2. Same S/N letter, but opposite E/I and opposite J/P: **Perfect pair (찰떡)**.
3. Same S/N letter: **Easy chemistry**.
4. Different S/N letter, same T/F letter: **Spark**.
5. Everything else: **Extra effort**.

| | Tier | Hearts | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 👯 | Twins | ♥♥♥♥ | Instant understanding. You'll also share the same blind spots. |
| 💞 | Perfect pair (찰떡) | ♥♥♥♥♥ | You see the world the same way and fill each other's gaps. |
| 😊 | Easy chemistry | ♥♥♥♥ | Same wavelength on what's worth talking about. |
| ⚡ | Spark | ♥♥♥ | Different worlds, shared values. Expect great debates. |
| 🧗 | Extra effort | ♥♥ | Different on both what you notice and how you decide. Worth it if you're curious. |

## How an agent uses it

- **Best-match persona:** one of the person's two perfect-pair types. Pick between them using the style the person preferred in the taste test.
- **Wildcard:** a Spark type, for someone who wants to be challenged, or their Twin, for someone who wants to feel understood.
- **Explaining the chemistry:** combine the four letter lines below into two or three sentences, then add the tier's one-liner.
- Never present Extra effort as bad. It just takes more translating, and some charts (socionics) say total opposites are the ideal match.

## Letter chemistry

One line per letter, depending on whether the two codes share it.

| Letters | Same | Different |
|---|---|---|
| E/I | E: You both think out loud. / I: You both like time to think first. | One brings the energy, the other the focus. |
| S/N | S: You both like facts and specifics. / N: You both love big ideas and what-ifs. | One sees the details, the other the big picture. |
| T/F | T: You both go straight to fixing the problem. / F: You both care how everyone feels. | One fixes, one feels. Cue the "are you a T?" moment. |
| J/P | J: You both like a plan. / P: You both like to wing it. | One plans, one improvises. |

**Example, ENFP + INFJ:** One brings the energy, the other the focus. You both love big ideas and what-ifs. You both care how everyone feels. One plans, one improvises. You see the world the same way and fill each other's gaps.
